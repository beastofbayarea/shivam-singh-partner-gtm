## Evidence-grounded premise

The FTC requires advertising claims to be truthful, non-deceptive, and substantiated. The World Bank's impact-evaluation handbook explains counterfactual and causal evaluation. These sources support market-development funds governed by approved claims, incremental partner contribution, and observable buyer progression rather than reported activity alone.

## Case approach

- Require a defined audience, buyer problem, substantiated proposition, owner, budget, and measurement design.
- Separate partner-sourced, partner-influenced, and incremental outcomes.
- Use matched comparisons or holdouts where feasible and reconcile claims to CRM and finance evidence.
- Release additional funds against evidence and close programs with unresolved compliance or data gaps.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use claim substantiation, incremental qualified pipeline, sales acceptance, fund utilization, evidence completeness, partner concentration, and disputed credit. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
