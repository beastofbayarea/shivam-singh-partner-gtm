# Partner Marketing Turnaround — MDF & Pipeline Yield

> **Portfolio lens:** MDF governance, partner activation, attribution, partner-sourced pipeline, and performance-based investment.

## Executive snapshot

Changed a $12M, 30-partner program from activity funding and lead volume to pipeline yield. Propensity-based sales allocation, protected nurture, common attribution, CRM traffic shaping, controlled holdouts, and tranche-based MDF connected partner investment to measurable revenue.

## Resume-ready impact

- Led a $12M partner-marketing turnaround across 30+ partners, replacing lead volume with attributable pipeline per dollar spent.
- Increased prioritized-lead conversion from 6.5% to 24% and reduced CRM API load 81% through propensity scoring, nurture, and value-based event routing.
- Used a 500-account holdout to support $50M in incremental GMV at 4.1x ROI and moved MDF from proof of execution to proof of performance.

## Interview story

### Situation

The program generated 12,000 monthly leads but only about 5% converted. Sellers spent time on low-intent contacts, systems crashed under event volume, and partners treated MDF as an entitlement.

### Task

Allocate scarce sales attention and partner funding according to expected revenue yield while protecting valuable exceptions and building auditable attribution.

### Actions

- Backtested a propensity threshold and preserved strategic-account overrides plus continuous re-scoring.
- Kept ten percent of low-scoring leads in a control to quantify false-negative risk.
- Standardized campaign taxonomy and used W-shaped attribution plus closed-won feedback.
- Released MDF in tranches tied to attributable pipeline and classified the shared data platform as partner enablement.

### Results

- Pilot conversion increased from 6.5% to 24%.
- API load fell 81%, and sales cycles reached 85 days.
- Targeted-account pipeline grew 38% versus 4% in holdout.
- The program isolated approximately $50M in incremental GMV at 4.1x ROI and funded half of platform cost centrally.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Accept fewer leads when sales-hour yield improves.
- Never delete low-scoring demand; nurture and re-score on new intent.
- Release partner funds against measurable milestones rather than campaign activity.

## Leadership signal

Made lower volume a sign of discipline by proving the rejected pool's quality, protecting a recovery path, and connecting every partner investment to shared revenue evidence.
