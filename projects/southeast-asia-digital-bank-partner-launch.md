>
> **Timeline alignment:** The [public Partner and Ecosystem GTM resume](https://github.com/beastofbayarea/shivam-singh-partner-gtm/blob/main/shivam-singh-partner-gtm.pdf) is used only to place this case within the McKinsey role dated July 2014-June 2016.

## Evidence-grounded premise

Global Findex 2014 documented both account growth and substantial remaining exclusion in East Asia and the Pacific. PAFI linked inclusion to safe transaction accounts, identification, interoperability, and broad access. FATF applied risk-based controls to mobile and internet payments, while MAS's 2016 sandbox consultation illustrated bounded experimentation. These sources support distribution partners as accountable operating participants.

## Case approach

- Map acquisition, identity, consent, underwriting inputs, payments, servicing, complaints, and exceptions by partner.
- Apply proportionate controls and explicit ownership across the full customer journey.
- Qualify partners through data, control, support, and escalation readiness.
- Scale only after bounded launch evidence meets customer, credit, operational, and regulatory gates.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use active account use, identity exceptions, payment success, credit quality, complaints, partner readiness, control breaches, and remediation time. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
