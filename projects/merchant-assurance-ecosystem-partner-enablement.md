## Evidence-grounded premise

PCI DSS 4.0 defines baseline requirements for protecting payment-account data and clarifies roles and validation. The World Bank Fast Payments Toolkit covers authentication, consumer protection, fraud, disputes, interoperability, and access. These sources support an assurance ecosystem in which partners have explicit responsibilities, evidence, escalation, and merchant-support obligations.

## Case approach

- Map obligations across merchants, payment providers, integrators, support partners, and platform owners.
- Convert standards into role-specific onboarding, evidence, exception, and remediation workflows.
- Segment partner enablement by technical access and operational responsibility.
- Tie expansion to observed readiness, not training attendance alone.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use evidence completeness, control exceptions, remediation aging, merchant incidents, dispute resolution, partner readiness, and repeat findings. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
